# Stage 2I futures practice

The authenticated Futures practice workspace trades three cash-settled contracts on Tape's existing synthetic Practice Index. Set `TAPE_PRACTICE_FUTURES_ENABLED=true` in a local `accounts,accounts-local` server after Flyway V9 and the [runtime grants](options-runtime-grants.sql). Use the schema-owner identity for migration and grants. The practice feature defaults to off. The pricing routine derives a synthetic futures mark from the existing index and a deterministic daily basis. Expiry uses the index level at expiry. Neither pricing routine contacts Upstox or uses exchange data.

## Money and margin

- The options and futures desks share the existing derivatives wallet of the user's demo account. Equity cash and positions have their own ledger. The first accepted derivatives order funds the wallet with ₹10,00,000 and journals the opening balance. Both desks show the shared cash, order reservations and collateral. Positions, orders and realized P&L are filtered by product.
- Lot size, multiplier, tick, margin and exposure limits come from the versioned specification. Initial margin applies to both long and short lots. Maintenance margin is the lower participation threshold, with no second deduction. The fixture uses ₹2,000 initial and ₹1,500 maintenance per lot. These figures are configurable through a new effective `derivative_specs` version and are illustrative. A position keeps its opening version through its close. A future with new economics requires closing the old position before extending exposure under the new version.
- Entry reserves collateral and posts the saved fee, with no notional cash exchange. `TAPE_PRACTICE_FUTURES_FEE_PAISE` defaults to 200 and accepts 1–10,000 paise. One resting order per contract reserves the fee and net increase in initial collateral for orders that can open exposure. Pure reductions need no separate order hold; the fill posts the fee and releases the applicable collateral. Mark-to-market posts signed price movement × units as variation cash and resets the remaining basis. Closing part of a position allocates basis proportionally and posts only P&L since that basis. Intraday unrealized P&L reflects the synthetic mark since the last basis. Realized P&L includes fees as incurred and posted variation gains and losses.
- Available funds are cash less posted initial collateral and open-order reserves. Maintenance shortfall measures how far posted cash falls below maintenance requirements plus reserves. Variation losses can leave cash or available funds negative. New exposure requires nonnegative available funds before and after the fill. Reduce-only orders are rechecked under the lock and may realize losses while in deficit. Cancellation and settlement remain available. There is no automatic liquidation or replenishment.

## Daily marks and recovery

V9 keeps existing derivative order IDs and financial records when it renames the order table. It adds the `reduce_only` order flag and an append-only `practice_future_sessions` calendar per futures contract. The calendar has a 15:30 IST mark every calendar day before expiry, including weekends. Each contract has one mark per applicable day and one cash settlement at expiry. These are practice sessions. Contract identity already distinguishes index and stock underlyings and cash versus physical settlement; only the practice cash-index source is eligible today.

The worker locates due calendars using the last immutable event for each open position. It posts missed marks under the account lock and before any new futures or options fill spends shared funds. A closed position has no further marks. At expiry, outstanding orders release their reservations and positions post the remaining variation against the final index price in one transaction. The existing journal uniqueness constraints make this safe across worker restarts. Session instants and prices are deterministic for this source version. A failed transaction can be retried from its prior journal entry. Readers show posted balances and label pending marks separately.

To pause submission and matching, set `TAPE_PRACTICE_FUTURES_SUBMISSIONS=false` and leave the feature enabled so reads, cancellations, daily marks and settlement continue. Drain positions and outstanding orders before a downgrade. A V8 application expects the previous order table name. A downgrade requires a reviewed forward compatibility migration and reconciliation of all V9 records. Keep a verified backup and the journal. Do not edit applied migrations.

## API and verification

`GET /api/v1/accounts/{id}/futures` returns contracts, shared balances, positions, order history and recent variation, fees and settlements. `POST /api/v1/accounts/{id}/futures/orders` accepts an account-scoped request key, contract and specification version, side, lots, optional limit and optional `reduceOnly`. `DELETE /api/v1/accounts/{id}/futures/orders/{orderId}` cancels an open order. Authentication, CSRF and same-account ownership apply to the workspace. Browser smoke testing can use the loopback profiles with a disposable database and the runtime role. Use `./mvnw verify -Dtape.postgres.tests=true` for automated accounting, race, restart, migration and authorization coverage.

The current implementation fills orders all-or-none and uses no order-book depth, official margins, holidays, stock-futures delivery, or live-data execution. Data entitlements, exchange contract rules and risk policy would need separate verification before connecting a real price source.
